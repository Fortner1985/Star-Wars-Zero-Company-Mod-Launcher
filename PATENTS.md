# Patent posture

## Plain English

This project is published so that it stays free.

Everything in this repository is released under Apache 2.0, which includes an
explicit patent grant from every contributor. Anyone may use it, modify it, and
ship it. Nobody — including the original author — can take what is published
here and use a patent to stop anyone else from using it.

Publishing it also makes it **prior art**. That means a third party cannot come
along later, patent one of these techniques, and demand that the community stop
using the thing it was already using.

## What that means for contributors

By contributing, you grant everyone a patent licence covering your contribution,
under Apache 2.0 §3. You keep your copyright.

Apache 2.0 §3 also terminates the patent licence of anyone who initiates patent
litigation alleging that this work infringes a patent. Contribute in good faith
and this never affects you.

## Scope

This statement covers the contents of this repository. Work by the author that
is not published here is not covered, and its absence should not be read as an
admission or a grant either way.

Not legal advice.
